What the numbers don't show

The calculator only counts what you budget. These costs arrive unbudgeted.

PCI exposureRunning card payments on an unpatched platform makes PCI-DSS compliance and breach liability your problem, not Adobe's.
The stack beneathM1 runs on PHP versions that are themselves end-of-life — the OS, PHP and platform all age together.
Extension vendors are goneMost M1 extension makers stopped shipping updates years ago. Every conflict is now a custom fix.
The hiring pool shrinksFewer developers know M1 every year. Scarcity sets their rate — and your bus factor.
Methodology, sources and honesty notes

All defaults are editable examples, not quotes or verified market rates. Replace them with your own hosting, maintenance and migration figures for a real comparison.

Staying cost = hosting ×12 + security patching + maintenance & development + extension fixes (+ optional revenue at risk). Moving cost = one-time migration ÷ the amortisation period + ongoing hosting, maintenance, patches, licence and renewals.

Break-even = migration cost ÷ (M1 yearly cost − M2 ongoing cost). Shown only when M2's ongoing cost is actually lower; otherwise the case for moving rests on security and risk, and the calculator says so.

Magento 1 reached end of life in June 2020: no official security patches, no quality fixes, PCI attestations increasingly hard to maintain. The "patching" and elevated "maintenance" lines are the operational cost of that fact.
